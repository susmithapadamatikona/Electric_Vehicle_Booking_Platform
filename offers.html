The fine print

How our offers work

One promo code per booking. Automatic discounts — like the 12% long-rental reduction — do stack on top of a promo code, and we always apply the combination that costs you least.
Discounts apply to the rental subtotal before tax. Taxes are calculated on the discounted amount, so a promo code reduces your GST too. Security deposits are never discounted — they're refundable in full.
Cancel within the free window and single-use codes are returned to your account for next time. Cancel outside it and the code is consumed, though support can usually reinstate it once as a goodwill gesture.
Referral credit is valid for 90 days from the date it lands in your account. It applies automatically at checkout, oldest credit first, so nothing goes to waste.
