About EVRide

We're removing every excuse not to drive electric

Range anxiety. Charger roulette. Opaque rental pricing. The reasons people give for staying with petrol are real problems — and every one of them is a software problem as much as a hardware one.

EVRide exists to solve them in one connected system: honest live range, reservable charging, transparent pricing and a car that shows up charged when it said it would.

Our mission

Make the electric option the easy option

Transport is roughly 14% of India's emissions. We think the fastest way to move that number isn't persuading people to care — it's making electric simply better to use than the alternative.

Honest by default

We publish live range, not lab figures. Prices include tax. If a car is at 62%, the app says 62% — even when a rounder number would sell better.

Charging is part of the product

A rental company that ignores charging has solved half the problem. We run our own network and integrate partner sites so one app covers the trip.

Measured, not claimed

Every CO₂ figure we publish is computed per trip from real distance and the grid mix at the time — audited annually, never estimated upward.

Owners share the upside

A third of our fleet belongs to individuals who list their EVs with us. They keep 82% of every booking and control their own pricing.

Safety isn't an add-on

Insurance and roadside assistance are included on every booking, not sold as an upsell at the counter when you have no leverage.

Clean energy, actually

72% of the electricity across our network is renewable, through direct PPAs rather than unbundled certificates. Target is 100% by 2028.

Our story

From one borrowed hatchback to a national network

2021

A frustrating weekend in Coorg

Our founders rented an EV for a weekend trip and spent four hours hunting for a working charger. The car was fine. Everything around the car was broken.

2022

Twelve cars, one spreadsheet

EVRide launched in Indiranagar with twelve vehicles and a booking system held together by a shared sheet. The first 500 trips taught us more than a year of planning had.

2023

The charging network begins

We stopped waiting for infrastructure to arrive and built our own — eight sites across Bengaluru, then partnerships that took the usable network past 600 points.

2024

Owners join the fleet

Individual EV owners began listing their cars. Supply grew four times in a year without us buying a single extra vehicle, and owners started earning from an asset that used to sit idle.

2025

Corporate and real-time telemetry

Live vehicle data went platform-wide, and the corporate product launched with 140 business accounts in its first nine months.

2026

Fourteen cities and counting

18,500 vehicles, 2,400 charging points, and 1.8 million kilograms of CO₂ that never made it into the air. Two more cities land this quarter.
