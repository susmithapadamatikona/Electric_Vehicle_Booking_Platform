Charging Explained

Which charger do you need?

Charging speed depends on the connector, the charger and what your car can accept. Here's the short version.

AC — up to 22 kW

Slow and cheap. Best overnight or while you're parked for hours. A typical 60 kWh pack takes 6–9 hours from near empty.

Type 2 ₹11–14 / kWh

DC Fast — 50–150 kW

The everyday workhorse. Roughly 10% to 80% in 30–50 minutes — long enough for a coffee, short enough not to derail a trip.

CCS2 CHAdeMO ₹15–18 / kWh

Hyperhub — up to 350 kW

For 800-volt cars like the IONIQ 5, EV6 and Taycan: 10% to 80% in about 18 minutes. Slower cars still charge at their own peak rate.

800V ready ₹20–24 / kWh
